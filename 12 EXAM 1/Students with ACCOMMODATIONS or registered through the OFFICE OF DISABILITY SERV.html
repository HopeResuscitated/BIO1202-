Students with ACCOMMODATIONS or registered through the OFFICE OF DISABILITY SERVICES (ODS)

IF you are enrolled in Disability Service, please note the information below.  If you are NOT registered with Disability Services, then this information below does not apply to you:

Students that are enrolled in Disability Services (DS) should automatically have their accommodations on file with the new Disability Service website.  There is no longer the need to give me a copy of your accommodation form.  The DS website is designed to streamline the process for students and Instructors make the collection of information more centralized.  Other items to note include:

1. If you have an accommodation for a notetaker, someone will be assigned to you and send you the lecture notes.  Neither the student nor the notetaker will be identified, so the process is more anonymous.  If you are NOT receiving notes, then you should contact Disability Services and not me.

2. If you have an accommodation for taking exams (i.e. extended time, distraction reduced environment, etc.), then you will be required to schedule and take your exams THROUGH Disability Services.  They have very strict policies about reserving exam times at least 5 business days in advance, so please be sure of their guidelines.  If you take your exams through the LSU Testing Center, I can NOT guarantee you will have your accommodations in place (i.e. you will not have the extended time).  This policy is strictly enforced.

3. If there is something outside of your accommodation that I can assist with, please email me (if this is an ONLINE course) or see me directly before/after class (if this is an LIVE course) so that we can communicate exactly what those needs are.  Too often, things get lost in translation or misinterpreted if they are not clearly communicated between the two of us.

The information below is just a summary of information.  You should check out the LSU Disability Services website for more complete and up-to-date policies.  The website is listed below:

http://students.lsu.edu/disability

If you are formally registered through Disability Services, they should provide you with a formal document on their LSU letterhead explaining your general circumstances.  Please send me a copy of that document through email as soon as possible.  Depending on your circumstances, you may be entitled to:

1.  Extended time on assignments and exams
2.  Class notes (sent by a note taken who I have selected)
3.  Taking your exams in a distraction reduced environment
4.  Tape recorded lectures, use of a recorder
5.  Alternate exam testing options
6.  Consideration for spelling
7.  Consideration for absences
8.  Use of a reader for the pretest and/or exam
9.  Use of a scribe

This list is not a comprehensive one and there may be something else that you are entitled to that I am unaware of.  The most important point is that you communicate with me what you need and I will do my best to accommodate you as best I can under the LSU guidelines.

If this is your first time using Disability Services, please make sure that you are fully aware of their policies, especially when it comes to scheduling  exams.  You must complete and submit the Disability Examination Request form (myLSU login required) at least five (5) business days prior to the exam. Your instructor must submit the completed request via email confirmation to Disability Services.  Examination Request Forms received less than five business days prior to the examination will not be accepted.

When you are requesting an exam, you MUST INDICATE WHICH ASSESSMENT you are requesting.
