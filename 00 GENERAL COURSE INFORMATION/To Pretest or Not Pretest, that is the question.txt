To Pretest* or Not Pretest, that is the question...

*NOTE: A pretest may not be offered this semester due to space limitations in the Testing Center.  Instead, you can generate a PLETHORA of review materials using Mastering Biology (as detailed on MOODLE in our course)

I can not stress enough the importance of taking the pretest.  It is essentially a no-penalty run at the real exam.  The pretest pulls from the SAME pool of questions as the real exam, has roughly the same number of questions as the real exam and is in the same format as the real exam.

If you end up doing not as well on the exam and did NOT take the pretest, you have no one but yourself to start blaming...
