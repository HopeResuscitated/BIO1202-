Yes! Since we use computer based testing, you can schedule your exam on any day/time
within the testing window (typically exams use a 3 day window and pretests use a 2 day window).

The beauty about computer based testing is that I can ask you a wide variety of question types
(such as M/C, True/False, matching, ranking, drag & drop, interactive, color, audio, video, etc.).
It is a MUCH BETTER testing mechanism than traditional scantron exams where there is typically
just one correct answer (A, B, C, D or E).

Another plus about computer based testing is that you will have your score displayed as soon as you 
are done iwth your session and press "SUBMIT".

Your score may have bonus added to it or there may be a curve on the exam, so the score that you
see posted to your MOODLE GRADEBOOK is typically higher than the score that you see in the Testing
Center if you took advantage of all the possible bonus activities (such as Word of the Days, Surveys,
MOODLE activities, etc.).
