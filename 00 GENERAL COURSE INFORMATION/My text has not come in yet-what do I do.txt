My text has not come in yet-what do I do?

Based on my conversation with the textbook repsy, this is what I recommend...

1. If you purchased the textbook package from the LSU bookstore (for ~$160), you can go
there NOW and they will give you the Mastering Biology access card (that includes the e-text,
so you will have access to reading the chapters).  Once the physical text comes in, simply
show them your receipt and they will give you the physical textbook to complete your order. 
All transactions/inquiries should go through the CUSTOMER SERVICE desk at the LSU bookstore.

2. If you already have a used text (or had borrowed one from me), then you still need the
Mastering Biology access code.  You can do that by going onto masteringbiology.com and purchasing
JUST the code for our textbook.  Be sure to look at the instructional powerpoint that I posted
to MOODLE for instructions.

3. If you have not purchased anything yet for our course, then you can go to the LSU bookstore
and they are selling the Mastering Biology access code for ~$110 and that combo INCLUDED the
e-text (so you will have access to reading the chapters).

So...one of three three options should work for everyone in terms of getting the required course
materials.
