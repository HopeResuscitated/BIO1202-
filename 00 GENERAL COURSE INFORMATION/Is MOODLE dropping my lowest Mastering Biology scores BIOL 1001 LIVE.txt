Is MOODLE dropping my lowest Mastering Biology scores?

We drop 10% of the lowest scores for Mastering Biology assignments.  This can be any
combination of quizzes or homeworks.  I know, historically, that this will end up being
around 3-4 scores.  So, I set MOODLE up to DROP the lowest 4 scores right now.  That is why
you may have a few zeros for assignments, but still have close to a 100% average.

This way, you will always know what your EXACT average is for the MB homework/quiz
portion of your grade (which accounts for 10% of your overall grade in BIOL 1001).

MOODLE now does INDICATE which scores are being dropped in your grade book, sp trust
me...it is accurate.
