How much time do I have to take the exam?

The Testing Center will allow you a MAXIMUM of 2 hours.  However, the average time that students
use is about 50-60 minutes.

Usually the proctors will ask you to finish up your exam at around the 90 minute mark, especially
if you are the last testing session/slot for the day.

If you know the material and have studied the materials I provided (notes, review questions, review
picture file, etc.), you should need about 45 minutes to complete the exam.

If you have an accomodation through Disabilty Services, then you are most likely given extended time
for your exam while taking it over in Johnston Hall.  Since each persons case is different, you 
should make sure you are fully aware of their policies.  For more information, please see the web site:

http://students.lsu.edu/disability
