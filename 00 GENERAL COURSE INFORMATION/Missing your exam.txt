Help!  I missed my exam...

If you miss your exam or just never scheduled it, this is what you need to do:

1.  Immediately go online and try to secure a new reservation.

2.  If nothing is available, keep checking again and again.  You may need to check immediately after midnight for an exam time for the following day (if the exam window is still running and open).

3.  Go directly to the Testing Center and see if there is an available seat.  Many times that have no-shows and you will be able to use one of these seats to take your exam.

4.  If you fail to take the exam within the assigned window, you will end up with a zero.  End of discussion.  Period.  No exceptions.

It is amazing how many people get "sick" the lat day of the exam window and are not able to make their appointment.  Be aware that if you do not have supporting documents from a verifiable professional, you risk getting a zero (0%) on an exam.
