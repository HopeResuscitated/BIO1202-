Can I start and stop a homework or quiz while in Mastering Biology, or do I need to finish
it complete and submit in one session/sitting?

You CAN stop and start any Mastering Biology homework or quiz and come back to it later (as
long as it is not overdue or closed out).  Just be sure to finish all parts WITHIN a particular
question for the assignment (if it is a multi-part question) and SUBMIT that question before
logging out.

If there are still some questions that you have not attempted, you can log back in at a later
time and finish the assignment.
