I just added the course.  Will I be able to see all of the emails that you sent to the class before I added?

Unfortunately, the answer to that is no.  The email history shows your personal history.  That means users (you the student) can only see the email they sent via Moodle.  In other words, for the students who added late, they won’t be able to see the emails that had already been sent.
