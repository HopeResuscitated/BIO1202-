For almost all of the chapters, I have a crossword puzzle (CWP).

The CWPs can be a great activity to help you learn the terminology for a chapter.
Since Biology is like a foreign language to many of you, if you learn the specific
terms as we work through a chapter, it will help you pull together the "big picture"

The CWPs are NOT required, but in many instances I will use them as bonus points on an exam.

If that is the case, I will announce in lecture that I am collecting CWPs on a
particular date and you must PHYSICALLY turn them in at the end of lecture that day.

Please STAPLE (NO paperclips, thumb tacks, etc.) them together if you are turning in
more than one.  You will need to print out the CWPs and I will only collect the puzzle
sheet, not the clues page.  Your name must be CLEARLY written on the sheet and NO late
submission will be accepted.

As for "how much bonus you will earn for turning these in?", I have no idea.  It all
depends on what the overall exam average is.  And, since I am not a psychic (if I was,
I would have hit the Powerball a LONG time ago and living in a mansion on a Caribbean
Island sipping margaritas every day...), please do not ask "...how many points will I
get for tuning the CWPs in?"
