The score I saw at the Testing Center is higher than what I see posted to MOODLE.  WHAT HAPPENED?

When you are done with your exam and press "SUBMIT", the computer will show you two scores.

One score is your RAW SCORE (i.e. 75/118) and the other is your PERCENTAGE SCORE (i.e. 63.6%).

I use exact scores when calculating grades (i.e. 79.6%) whereas the Testing Center will show you rounded up scores (i.e. 80%).
