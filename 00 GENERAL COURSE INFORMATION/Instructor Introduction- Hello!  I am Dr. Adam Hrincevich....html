In order to do well in this course, it is very important to keep up with the materials. I know that many of you have families, pets, full-time (in addition to part-time) jobs, so you'll need to figure out how to work this course into your busy schedules. As your Instructor, it is my role to help you get through the course in terms of answering questions, addressing material you are having trouble understanding, providing feedback to discussion posts, etc.  But in order to get the most out of this course, you need to put your time and effort into it. Historically, I have found that people do the best if they try and dedicate a set period of time each week to the class and dedicate themselves to that schedule.
